What is Solipsistic?

A creed of self-reliance.

Solipsistic takes its name from solipsism — the philosophy that the self is the one true certainty. It is a creed of individuality and independence, long debated by thinkers as the purest study of self-awareness.

In Aeternum, we are a company for those who walk their own road — yet leave a wake that others can't ignore. Whether you're a lone wolf chasing glory, an explorer unraveling the island's secrets, or a strategist amassing a war chest, this is a banner that answers to no one but its own.
